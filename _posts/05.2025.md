**:::: Tuesday, May 14, 4-6pm, @ Digital Humanities Lab ::::**

Emute Lab is meeting May 14 with presentations from Steve Symons and Chris Kiefer.

Steve will present (and you can play with) his current work called The Light That Entangles (TLTE): a 3 person installation instrument based around audio-modulated light and solar cells.  Steve will talk about how he has been querying the use of a diffractive focussed design methodology that explores the tension between human centred Tangible and Embodied Interaction and post-human theories of Entanglement.

Chris will update us on the recent developments regarding his Machine Learning sound module.

Everyone is welcome at this meeting, looking forward to seeing you.
