Or should this be a 'lunchNIME' concert?  We present a line up of experimental music acts, all of whom will be playing similar sets at this years [NIME](https://nime2026.org/) conference in London, later in June.

**The line up:**

***Noise Peddler***
A symmetrical duo of no-input pedalboards

***Bunty x Furrowed***
Record cutting / vocals  / FX

***Betty Accorsi & Chris Kiefer***
AI Augmented Sax and Feedback Strings

Weds June 10th @ The Meeting House
